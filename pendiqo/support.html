Pendiqo Support

Help with Nudges, recurrence, snoozing, completion, and local notifications.

Frequently asked questions

Why didn’t I receive a notification?

Confirm that notifications and sounds are enabled for Pendiqo, the task’s reminder is in the future, Stop Future Nudges is not selected, and Silent Mode, Focus, or Notification Summary is not suppressing or delaying delivery.

Why is the notification quiet?

Pendiqo includes a custom sound, but iOS controls final playback. Check notification volume, Silent Mode, Focus settings, and Pendiqo’s sound permission.

Does Pendiqo work without internet?

Yes. Tasks, recurrence, snoozing, completion, storage, and notifications work locally without an internet connection.

Does Pendiqo sync between devices?

No. Pendiqo has no cloud or cross-device synchronization, remote backup, import, or export.

What happens when I complete a repeating task?

The completed occurrence stays in Completed, and Pendiqo creates the next valid occurrence with its own notification schedule.

Can I stop reminders without completing a task?

Yes. Choose Stop Future Nudges. The task remains incomplete and visible, but its pending notifications are cancelled until its schedule changes.

Notification limits

iOS limits the number of pending local notifications. Pendiqo manages an internal budget of up to 50 notifications and prioritizes primary reminders over optional follow-ups. The app warns when a primary reminder cannot fit within that budget.

Important limitations

Pendiqo is not a medical device, medication-adherence guarantee, emergency-alert system, guaranteed alarm, calendar replacement, or cloud backup service. Do not rely on it as the sole reminder for critical, medical, safety-related, legal, or emergency matters.
