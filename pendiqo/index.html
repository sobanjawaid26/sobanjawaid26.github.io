Reminder intensity

Choose how firmly
Pendiqo follows up.

Every Nudge can use one of three locally scheduled reminder styles. You remain in control, and future notifications can be stopped without marking the task complete.

Once

One notification at the selected reminder time.

Follow Up

A primary notification and one follow-up if the task remains unfinished.

Until Done

A primary notification with several spaced follow-ups while the task still needs attention.

Important: Pendiqo is not a guaranteed alarm, medical device, medication-adherence guarantee, or emergency-alert system. Notification delivery depends on iOS permissions and device settings.
