Pendiqo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Pendiqo is a local reminder app. Your Nudges and notification schedules are managed on your device and are not sent to the developer.

Data stored by Pendiqo

Stored task information can include a title, task type, due date and time, reminder time and preference, recurrence rule, selected weekdays, reminder intensity, completion status and time, snooze time, temporary Doing Now state, future-Nudge status, recurrence relationships, and creation date.

Local storage and notifications

Pendiqo stores task data locally using Apple SwiftData and schedules reminders through Apple’s local notification framework. Background refresh may be used for local notification maintenance; it does not upload or download data.

No collection or networking

Pendiqo has no account, login, subscription, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDK, tracking technology, third-party authentication, payment processor, external database, remote server, or network API call. The developer does not collect, sell, share, or upload task information.

Permissions

Pendiqo may request permission for notification alerts, sounds, and badges. It does not request unrelated access such as location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, health data, or tracking.

Data deletion and transfer

You can delete individual tasks inside Pendiqo. Removing the app deletes its local data according to normal iOS behavior. Pendiqo does not currently provide cloud synchronization, cross-device synchronization, remote backup, data export, or data import.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Pendiqo’s functionality or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.
