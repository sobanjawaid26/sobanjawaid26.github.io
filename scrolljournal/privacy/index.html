Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ScrollJournal handles information in the iPhone app and on this website. ScrollJournal is provided by MD SOBAN JAWAID.

1. Information stored locally

ScrollJournal stores the journal information you enter locally on your device. This can include session dates, platform names, time spent, reasons, moods before and after scrolling, built-in or custom tags, Daily Reflections, and locally generated Insights.

The app uses Apple SwiftData for journal storage and UserDefaults for the locally stored custom-tag catalogue.

2. No collection or transmission of journal data

ScrollJournal does not transmit journal information off your device. The developer cannot access, retrieve, read, recover, sell, or share your sessions, platform names, reasons, moods, tags, reflections, or generated Insights.

Common platform suggestions and recently used names are local conveniences. The app does not connect to social-media accounts, use their APIs, read browsing history, access Apple Screen Time, or automatically detect app usage.

3. Accounts, payments, analytics, advertising, and third parties

ScrollJournal does not require an account or sign-in and does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party libraries, third-party SDKs, third-party APIs, or a developer-operated server. It does not sell or share personal information and does not provide developer-operated cloud synchronization.

4. Locally generated Insights

Weekly and monthly Insights are produced on your device from the structured information you manually record. They use deterministic calculations, not an external AI service. These summaries are personal reflection tools and are not medical, psychological, diagnostic, therapeutic, or professional advice.

5. Optional notifications

If you choose to enable Daily Reflection reminders, ScrollJournal requests notification permission contextually. Apple UserNotifications schedules those reminders locally through iOS. Notification content is not sent to the developer. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Storage, retention, and deletion

Journal information remains locally on your device until you delete it or remove the app. You can delete individual sessions inside ScrollJournal. The developer cannot recover deleted journal information.

Removing ScrollJournal deletes its locally stored application data from that device, subject to any backups independently maintained by the device operating system.

7. Device backups

ScrollJournal does not implement its own cloud backup or synchronization. Your device operating system may handle backups separately according to your Apple and iOS backup settings. Those backup services are controlled by you and Apple, not by the ScrollJournal developer.

8. Support emails

If you voluntarily email support, the developer receives your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. That information is used only to answer your support request and meet legitimate legal or security obligations. Do not include private journal text unless it is necessary for your request.

9. Website privacy

This website does not use analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, a contact form, or cookies added by the developer. Its hosting provider may process standard technical request logs under its own practices. Using the email link opens your chosen email service; it does not submit information through this website.

10. Security

Keeping journal information on your device limits external access, but no storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. The security of locally stored information also depends on your device, passcode, operating-system security, and backup settings.

11. Children’s privacy

ScrollJournal is a general-audience journaling utility and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect or transmit journal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information through a support email may contact the developer to request its deletion.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if ScrollJournal’s features or data practices change. Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
