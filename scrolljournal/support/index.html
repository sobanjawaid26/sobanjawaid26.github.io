Frequently asked questions

How do I create a session?

From Today, choose Add session. Enter a platform name, time spent, reason for opening, mood before and after, and between one and three tags, then choose Save session. You can create multiple sessions for the same day.

How do I enter a custom platform?

Type any name in the platform field. You are not limited to the suggested names. The text you save becomes the platform name for that session.

How do common and recently used platform suggestions work?

Suggestions are neutral, text-only shortcuts shown from a built-in list or names you previously entered. They are generated locally. ScrollJournal does not contact a platform, access its API, or connect to your account.

How do I edit or delete a session?

Open a recorded session to reach Edit session. Change the details and choose Save changes, or choose Delete and confirm Delete Session. Deleted journal information cannot be recovered by support.

How do I create and manage custom tags?

In Add session or Edit session, open Custom tags. You can create reusable tags and rename or delete saved custom tags there. Each session must contain between one and three built-in or custom tags.

How do Daily Reflections work?

You can write one Daily Reflection for each day. A reflection must contain between 10 and 300 characters and is associated with the day rather than a specific session. Today’s reflection can be edited.

Why does yesterday’s reflection close at noon?

Yesterday remains available until 12:00 PM local time the following day so you have a short catch-up window while keeping each reflection tied to its day. After noon, the editing window closes and the app records that reflection day as completed or missed.

Why do Insights require at least three sessions?

ScrollJournal waits for at least three manually recorded sessions before generating a weekly or monthly review so a single entry is not presented as a broader pattern. Insights use deterministic calculations on your device and are personal reflection tools, not professional advice.

How do I enable or disable reminders?

You can choose Enable reflection reminders when the option appears in ScrollJournal. Permission is requested only after that choice, and reminders are scheduled locally through iOS. To disable them later, open iOS Settings, find ScrollJournal under Notifications, and turn off Allow Notifications.

Where is my journal information stored?

Sessions, platform names, reasons, moods, tags, custom tags, Daily Reflections, and generated Insights are stored locally on your iPhone. ScrollJournal does not provide developer-operated cloud sync.

Can the developer or support recover deleted information?

No. The developer and support cannot access or recover your locally stored journal. Deleted sessions cannot be restored by the developer.

What happens if I delete ScrollJournal?

Removing the app deletes its locally stored application data from that iPhone, subject to any backups independently maintained by the device operating system. ScrollJournal does not operate its own backup or recovery service.

Does ScrollJournal connect to social-media accounts?

No. It does not sign in to social-media services, access their APIs, read browsing history, monitor Apple Screen Time, or automatically detect which apps you use. Every session is entered manually.

Does ScrollJournal use AI or analytics?

No. ScrollJournal uses no external AI service, analytics, tracking, advertising, third-party libraries, or third-party SDKs. Insights are calculated locally using deterministic rules based only on the structured information you recorded.
