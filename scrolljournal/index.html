Built around what you enter

More context than
minutes alone.

01

Session logging

Create multiple sessions per day, edit them later, or delete them. Enter any platform name; neutral common suggestions and recently used names are local conveniences only.

02

Mood comparison

Record how you felt before and after each session, then review whether your selected mood improved, declined, or stayed the same.

03

Personal tags

Choose between one and three descriptive tags for a session. Use built-in options or create reusable custom tags that stay on your device.

04

Daily Reflections

Write one 10–300 character reflection for each day. Today remains editable, and yesterday remains open until noon the following day.

05

Weekly and monthly Insights

After at least three recorded sessions, review deterministic summaries calculated locally from the structured information you entered.

06

Optional reminders

If you enable them, iOS schedules local Daily Reflection reminders in the evening and the following morning before the deadline.

Personal reflection tools

Patterns, calculated privately.

Weekly and monthly Insights can summarize completed sessions, duration, intentional reasons, mood changes, streaks, reflections, and frequently recorded platforms, reasons, and tags.

Insights use deterministic calculations on your device. They do not use an external AI service and are not medical, psychological, diagnostic, therapeutic, or professional advice.

  • SessionsSee counts, total minutes, and daily averages.
  • IntentReview how often you recorded an intentional reason.
  • MoodCompare the mood choices entered before and after sessions.
  • PatternsNotice repeated associations in your own structured entries.
