Built around what you enter

More context than
minutes alone.

01

Flexible session logging

Record multiple sessions for today or an earlier date. Future dates are unavailable, and session duration can range from 1 to 1,440 minutes.

02

Any platform

Type a platform name or use local suggestions. Common names are normalized, recently used platforms appear first, and no platform account or API is contacted.

03

Reason and mood

Record why you opened a platform and select a five-point mood before and after. Scroll Journal classifies the result as improved, unchanged, or declined.

04

Personal tags

Add one to three built-in or custom tags. Reusable custom tags can be renamed or removed without changing their labels in historical sessions.

05

Daily Reflections

Write one 10–300 character reflection per day. Today remains editable, and yesterday remains open until noon the following day.

06

Earlier days

Browse daily totals, reflection previews, mood outcomes, reasons, and tag counts. Historical sessions remain editable and deletable.

Creator Library

Keep useful creators
worth revisiting.

A creator name or @handle is the only required field. You can optionally add a screenshot, profile link, and personal note, then search saved creators by available names and metadata.

The iOS Photos picker provides only the image you select. Scroll Journal resizes large images, converts them to locally stored JPEGs, and lets you replace or remove them.

  • PrivateCreator records and screenshots remain in local app storage.
  • Optional detailsAdd a screenshot, safe HTTP or HTTPS profile link, or personal note.
  • SearchableSearch names, existing handle and platform metadata, notes, and existing topics.
  • Under your controlEdit or delete a creator and its associated local information.

Personal reflection tools

Patterns, calculated privately.

Switch between weekly and monthly periods to review sessions, duration, intentional reasons, mood changes, streaks, reflections, daily averages, frequently recorded platforms, reasons, tags, and weekdays.

Narrative observations require at least three completed sessions. Period comparisons require at least three sessions in both periods, and repeated evidence is required before Scroll Journal makes a platform-level negative observation.

Insights use deterministic calculations on your device. They are not produced by an AI model or sent to an external service.

  • OverviewSee session counts, average duration, intentional use, and leading platforms.
  • Positive changesNotice improvements in mood, duration, purpose, or lower-mood outcomes.
  • PatternsReview repeated reasons, tags, platform outcomes, and longer distracted sessions.
  • Gentle focusReceive evidence-based suggestions grounded only in your own entries.
