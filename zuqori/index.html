Zuqori helps you save only the names of movies, books, TV shows, games, places, and more—not the content itself.

It does not stream, download, reproduce, or automatically fetch third-party content, artwork, descriptions, ratings, or reviews.

Private by design

No account.
No tracking. No AI.

Zuqori stores Queue information locally using Apple’s on-device technologies. There are no analytics, advertisements, third-party SDKs, or external recommendation services.
