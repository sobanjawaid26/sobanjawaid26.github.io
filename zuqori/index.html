Zuqori helps you save only the names of movies, books, TV shows, games, places, and more—not the content itself.

It does not provide, stream, reproduce, download, scrape, or automatically retrieve third-party content, restaurant information, artwork, descriptions, ratings, or reviews.

Everything you want to try

One private place.
Your choices, your context.

01

Save an idea

Save the name of something you want to try in your list. Choose a category and optionally add a personal note, link, source, and With or When context.

02

Choose what is next

Search, filter by category, sort by Recently Added, Oldest, or A–Z, and manage a focused Up Next shortlist.

03

Remember the experience

After marking an item as Tried, optionally select a private reaction, add a personal review note, or do both. You can also return it to your Queue.

Useful beyond a list

Organize now.
Decide when it matters.

01

One Queue

Keep the names you save together in one list, organized as Movie, TV Show, Book, Game, Restaurant / Food, Place, Activity, or Other.

02

Search and organize

Search across saved information, apply category filters, and sort by Recently Added, Oldest, or A–Z.

03

Focused Up Next

Maintain and visibly reorder a shortlist with a configurable limit from one to ten items.

04

Pick for Me

Make a local, non-AI random selection from names already saved in your Queue. Exact filters apply, and items already Tried or in Up Next are excluded. No external recommendation service is contacted.

05

Tried history

Reactions and personal review notes are optional and independent. A note saved without a reaction remains visible in the item’s details.

06

Duplicate warnings

Receive an on-device warning for matching normalized links, identical normalized titles, or narrowly matched similar longer titles. View the existing item or continue intentionally; no external database or API is used.

07

Your appearance

System is the default appearance, with Light and Dark also available. Your selection applies to both the splash screen and the main app interface.

Private by design

No account.
No tracking. No AI.

Core organizer features work offline. Zuqori stores Queue information locally on your iPhone and does not send it to a Zuqori server. Apple may include local app data in device or iCloud backups depending on your Apple settings. Zuqori has no analytics, advertising, third-party SDKs or APIs, remote configuration, or external recommendation service.
