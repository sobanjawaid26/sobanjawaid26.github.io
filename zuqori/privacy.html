Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: August 27, 2026

Zuqori is a private, offline personal Queue developed by MD SOBAN JAWAID. The developer does not collect data from the app.

Information you may enter

You may voluntarily save item titles, categories, notes, web links, discovery sources, people and time contexts, Up Next status and order, timestamps, Tried dates, reactions, and personal review notes. This information can reveal personal preferences, but the developer does not receive it.

Local storage

Zuqori stores Queue information locally on your device using Apple SwiftData. Preferences such as appearance and the Up Next limit use Apple UserDefaults. The app does not transmit saved Queue information to Zuqori or to a Zuqori-operated server. Zuqori has no account system or backend, so the developer cannot remotely view, retrieve, recover, or delete your Queue.

Data collection, tracking, and sharing

Zuqori and MD SOBAN JAWAID do not collect, transmit, sell, or share app data. Zuqori has no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, remote configuration, data brokers, or third-party SDKs. It does not build advertising profiles or track users across apps or websites.

Third parties and Apple services

Zuqori uses Apple system frameworks such as SwiftUI, UIKit, SwiftData, Foundation, and UserDefaults. No external recommendation service is used.

App data may be included in device or iCloud backups performed by Apple’s operating system, depending on the user’s Apple settings. These backups are controlled by Apple and the user, not by Zuqori. Zuqori does not control or guarantee backup or restoration through Apple’s services.

User-entered links

You may save and choose to open a web link. Zuqori does not automatically fetch content from saved links. The destination opens outside Zuqori and may have its own privacy practices, which Zuqori does not control.

Storage and synchronization

Zuqori does not provide its own backup, export, import, cloud storage, or synchronization service. Information entered in Zuqori is stored locally using Apple’s on-device storage technologies.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on the device until the user deletes individual items, removes the app and its local data, erases the device, or the information is otherwise removed through Apple’s operating-system controls.

Because Zuqori does not operate an account or backend server, the developer cannot remotely recover or delete information stored on a user’s device.

Permissions

Zuqori does not require location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, Bluetooth, health, tracking, or notification permission.

Security

Zuqori relies on protections provided by iOS and your device. Protect your device with a passcode. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
