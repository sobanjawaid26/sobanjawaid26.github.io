Zuqori

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Overview

Zuqori is designed to keep your saved experiences private and on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or external recommendation services.

Data stored by the app

Information you enter—such as item titles, categories, notes, links, tags, context, completion status, and reactions—is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device storage frameworks.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your queue data or personal information. Zuqori does not send your saved content to developer-operated servers or third-party recommendation services.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual items within Zuqori. Removing the app from your device also removes its locally stored app data, subject to the behavior of iOS and any device backups you control.

Third-party links

Items may contain links that you choose to save. Opening a link takes you to an external app or website, whose own privacy practices apply.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Zuqori’s features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.
