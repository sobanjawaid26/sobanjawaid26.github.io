Frequently asked questions

Does Zuqori require an account?

No. Zuqori does not require registration, login, subscription, or an internet connection for its core features.

Where is my information stored?

Saved items and preferences are stored locally using Apple’s on-device technologies. Zuqori does not operate a server containing your Queue.

Does Zuqori download movies, books, games, or other content?

No. Zuqori stores only names and optional information you enter. It does not provide, stream, reproduce, or download the content itself.

Does Pick for Me use AI?

No. Pick for Me runs locally and selects from your own eligible saved items using the filters you choose.

How do I add or edit something?

From Queue, tap Add Something, enter a title, select a category, and save. Open an item from Queue or Tried and tap Edit to change it.

How do I mark something as Tried?

Open an item or use its Queue controls, choose Mark as Tried, and optionally select a reaction and add a review note.

Can I change a reaction or move an item back?

Yes. Open a Tried item to change its reaction or review. Move Back to Queue clears its Tried date, reaction, and review note and returns it to your regular Queue.

How does Up Next work?

Up Next is a prioritized shortlist. Add or remove items, reorder them, use accessible Move Up or Move Down actions, and configure a limit between one and ten in Settings.

Why did Pick for Me find nothing?

Pick for Me follows your filters exactly and excludes Tried and Up Next items. Change Type, With, or When, or add more eligible items.

How do I find a saved item?

Use search in Queue or Tried. Category and reaction filters can narrow the results further.

Backup and device transfer

How do I export a backup?

Open Settings, choose Export All Items, and select where to save the JSON file.

How do I import a backup?

Open Settings, select Import JSON Backup, choose a valid Zuqori JSON file, then select Merge with Current Items or Replace All Current Items. Replace All permanently removes current items before importing.

Does Zuqori sync between devices?

Zuqori has no cloud-sync service. You can manually transfer a JSON backup. Apple device backup and restore behavior depends on your Apple settings.

Can the developer recover deleted information?

No. Zuqori has no account or server copy. Recovery is possible only from a backup you control.

Report a problem

Email sobanmuhammad26@icloud.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, Zuqori version and build from Settings, a description, and reproduction steps. Include a screenshot only when appropriate. Do not email private notes or complete JSON backups unless specifically necessary.
