MyQueue

Support

Help with saving, choosing, and remembering the things you want to try.

How do I add something?

Tap the add button from your Queue, enter a title, choose a category, and save. You can optionally include a link, notes, where you found it, who it is for, when it fits, and tags.

What is Up Next?

Up Next is a smaller priority list within your queue. Use it for the experiences you are most interested in trying soon. You can adjust its item limit in Settings.

How does Pick for Me work?

Choose optional filters for the type of experience, who you are with, and when it fits. MyQueue suggests an item from your own saved queue that matches those choices. It does not use an external recommendation service.

What happens after I try something?

Mark the item as tried and optionally record whether you loved it, thought it was okay, or decided it was not for you. The item remains available in your Tried history with its saved details.

Where is my data stored?

Your queue is stored locally on your device. MyQueue has no account, analytics, advertising, or external recommendation service.
