Frequently Asked Questions

Why am I not receiving notifications?

Open the iPhone Settings app, find Sessyn, select Notifications, and confirm that notifications are allowed. Also check Focus modes and notification-silencing settings. If notifications still do not appear, restart Sessyn and your iPhone.

Why has the widget not refreshed immediately?

Widget refresh timing is managed by iOS and updates may not always appear instantly. Open Sessyn to refresh its current information, then allow time for iOS to update the widget.

How do Live Activities work?

Live Activities can show an active timer on supported system surfaces. Availability and presentation depend on your device, iOS version, and system settings. Confirm that Live Activities are enabled for Sessyn in Settings if one does not appear.

Can I use Siri or Shortcuts?

Sessyn supports Siri and Shortcuts actions for supported timer tasks. Look for Sessyn actions in the Shortcuts app or ask Siri using a configured shortcut.

Where is my data stored?

Sessyn stores app data locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not provide cloud sync. See the Privacy Policy for details.
