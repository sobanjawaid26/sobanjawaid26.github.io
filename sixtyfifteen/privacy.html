Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Information We Collect

SixtyFifteen does not require you to create an account. The current version is designed to function primarily on your device and does not intentionally ask you to provide personal information through the app.

Local App Data

Focus session information and app preferences may be stored locally on your device to provide the app's features. This information remains subject to your device settings and actions, such as deleting the app or restoring your device.

Third-Party Services

The current version of SixtyFifteen does not intentionally integrate third-party analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, or advertising services. Apple may process information related to downloading, purchasing, or using apps under its own terms and privacy policy. If external services are added to SixtyFifteen later, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Data Sharing

SixtyFifteen is not currently designed to transmit or sell your locally stored focus session information or app preferences. Information may still be disclosed if required by applicable law or if necessary to protect legal rights.

Children's Privacy

SixtyFifteen is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the app, please contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised when the app changes or when legal and operational requirements evolve. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
