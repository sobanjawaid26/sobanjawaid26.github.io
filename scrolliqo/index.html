Weekly and monthly insights

Patterns, calculated privately.

After enough sessions or reflections, Scrolliqo can summarize duration, intentional use, streaks, mood outcomes, common platforms, reasons, tags, and changes from the previous period.

Gentle suggestions come from fixed, on-device rules—not artificial intelligence—and are intended for personal awareness, not medical advice.

  • IntentSee which reasons bring purpose to a session.
  • MoodCompare how you felt before and after scrolling.
  • PatternsNotice recurring platforms, tags, and behaviors.
  • ProgressCompare weekly or monthly periods over time.
