Scrolliqo Support

Help with manually recording sessions, completing Daily Reflections, and understanding your locally generated insights.

Frequently asked questions

Does Scrolliqo track social-media use automatically?

No. Session durations and details must be entered manually. Scrolliqo cannot detect when another app opens, import Apple Screen Time data, block apps, or impose usage limits.

Can I record more than one session per day?

Yes. You can record multiple sessions for today or previous dates. Future session dates cannot be selected.

How do Daily Reflections work?

You can complete one reflection per calendar day. Today’s reflection remains editable throughout the day. Yesterday’s reflection can be created or edited until 12:00 PM local time; after noon, that window closes.

Why are Insights not appearing?

The selected weekly or monthly period needs at least three completed sessions or three completed Daily Reflections. Insights are observational and are not medical advice.

Why did I not receive a reminder?

Confirm notifications are allowed for Scrolliqo in iOS Settings. Reminders are local and optional, and delivery depends on iOS notification permission and system behavior.

Data and account limitations

Scrolliqo has no account, cloud synchronization, bulk data export, or in-app “delete all data” control. Individual sessions can be deleted. Removing the app through iOS removes its local data unless independently preserved by an Apple device backup.
