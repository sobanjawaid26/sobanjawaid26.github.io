Scrolliqo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

Scrolliqo is designed as a private, on-device social-media journal. Sessions, reflections, and generated insights remain locally on your device.

Information stored by the app

Scrolliqo stores the sessions and Daily Reflections you enter. Session data can include the date, platform category, optional custom platform, manually entered duration, reason, optional custom reason, mood before and after, selected tags, and record timestamps. Reflection data can include the date, reflection text, status, and timestamps.

Locally generated insights

Counts, averages, percentages, streaks, mood changes, frequencies, period comparisons, and gentle focus suggestions are calculated on your device from saved sessions and reflections. Suggestions use fixed rules, not an external artificial-intelligence service.

No collection or external services

Scrolliqo does not require an account or internet connection. It has no advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, third-party libraries, external APIs, or developer-operated server. The developer does not collect, sell, or share your journal data.

No social-media or Screen Time access

Scrolliqo does not connect to social-media accounts or read messages, feeds, or account information. It does not access Apple Screen Time, automatically monitor app usage, detect when another app opens, block apps, or enforce usage limits.

Permissions

Scrolliqo may request notification permission for optional local reflection reminders. These reminders are scheduled using Apple’s on-device notification system. The app does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or payment information.

Deleting data

You can permanently delete individual sessions in the app. Scrolliqo currently has no account to delete and no bulk “delete all” control. Removing Scrolliqo through iOS removes locally stored app data unless it has independently been preserved by an Apple device backup.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Scrolliqo’s features or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.
