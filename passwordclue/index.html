Designed for privacy

Local by design.
Protected by iOS.

01

No account or cloud service

Core clue features work offline, with no sign-up, developer server, analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud sync.

02

Encrypted clue content

Username hints and clue layers are encrypted locally using AES-GCM.

03

Device authentication

Optionally unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode.

04

Layered reveals

Hints stay concealed and appear one at a time only when requested.

05

Local reminders

Optional on-device notifications help you review whether clues still work.

06

Privacy controls

Hide app-switcher content, cover active screen recordings, and lock quickly.

Original-device storage

Your clues stay on this device—and cannot be recovered by us.

Clues do not sync or transfer through backups. If the device is lost, damaged, or replaced, the developer cannot restore or decrypt them.
