Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026
Last updated: September 12, 2026

NudgeOnn is a local reminder app. Your items and notification schedules are managed on your device and are not transmitted to the developer.

Information stored on your device

NudgeOnn stores information needed to provide its reminder features, including user-entered item titles, dates and times, reminder settings, recurrence settings, and completion state. This information remains locally on your device.

Local storage and notifications

NudgeOnn stores item data locally using Apple’s SwiftData framework. It uses Apple’s UserNotifications framework to schedule and deliver local reminders. Notification permission is used only to deliver those local reminders.

Background refresh is used only as a best-effort mechanism to maintain pending local notifications. NudgeOnn reconciles pending reminders and respects iOS limits on pending local notifications.

No collection or transmission

NudgeOnn does not transmit your item data to the developer. It does not collect account information and requires no account or login.

NudgeOnn does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs, external APIs, a backend, or remote feature flags. It does not sell or share personal data.

Notification controls

Silent Mode, Focus, notification permissions, alert delivery, and notification sound settings are controlled by iOS. NudgeOnn includes a custom notification sound in the application bundle, but iOS determines whether and how a notification is presented.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual items inside NudgeOnn. You can remove all locally stored NudgeOnn data by deleting the application from your device.

NudgeOnn does not provide its own cloud backup or cross-device synchronization service.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if NudgeOnn’s functionality or data practices change. The last-updated date will be revised when an update is published.
