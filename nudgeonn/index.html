Reminder styles

Choose how NudgeOnn follows up.

NudgeOnn schedules local notifications through Apple’s UserNotifications framework and uses a custom sound included in the app.

Once

The primary notification only.

Follow Up

The primary notification and one follow-up if the item remains unfinished.

Until Done

The primary notification and additional follow-ups while the item remains unfinished.
